About these activities

The Advanced Interactive Activities follow directly from the MOOC Fundamentals of Ocean Forecasting and take you from learning to practice: working with real Copernicus Marine and ERA5 data for an African coastal region - learning how to access, understand, analyze, and interpret the data, and what they show.

They run in two parts: a series of live webinars, followed by a hands-on workshop. Both build day on day, so full attendance is required. The workshop runs on a cloud platform we provide - you do not need to install anything.

These activities are part of the OPERA Capacity Development Activities led by the ICMPA-UNESCO Chair (Universite d'Abomey-Calavi, Benin) and the Gulf of Guinea Ocean Sciences Summer School (GGOSSS). OPERA - Ocean Prediction Enhancement in the Regions of Africa - is implemented by Mercator Ocean International with EU funding. More about the project.
